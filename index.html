The problem

People don’t buy what they can’t picture on themselves.

Online, a shopper only ever sees the clothes on a model. Never on their own body. So they guess. Some guess wrong and send it back. Some don’t buy at all. Try-on takes the guessing out — they see it on themselves before they add to cart.

Common questions

What store owners ask us first.

How real does it actually look?

This is real-time video, not a generated photo. The garment tracks the shopper as they move, which is what makes it read as real instead of pasted on. Solid colours and simple patterns come out strongest; very detailed prints are still improving. Try it on yourself above — what you see is what your shoppers get.

Do I need a developer?

No. Openwear is a managed service — we do the setup for you. Book a 30-minute call and we take it from there. There is nothing for your team to build, and nothing to maintain afterwards.

Will it slow my store down?

No. Try-on is not part of your page load — it only starts when a shopper chooses to open it.

Does it work on phones?

Yes — mobile and desktop, using the camera the shopper already has. There is no app to download.

Which of my products can use it?

Tops, hoodies, knitwear and outerwear work best today. Solid colours and simple patterns come out strongest; very detailed prints and sheer fabrics are still improving.

What about my customers’ privacy?

The camera is requested on the shopper’s own device and only runs while they are using try-on.

What does it cost?

We do not have public pricing yet. Book a 30-minute call and we will work it out with you.
