For your engineers

What the one line of code gives you.

One line of code, live in under an hour

One line before </body>, or one click if you are on Shopify. No complex APIs, no build step, no theme files edited. Live in under an hour with near-zero engineering cost. Deleting the line removes it completely.

Fast performance, live video in real time

The try-on is live video, rendered in real time as your shopper moves. What sits on your storefront is a single async script of about 17 KB, so your pages keep loading exactly as fast as they do today.

Native by design

It disappears into your storefront: neutral surfaces, a subtle radius, no competing brand colour, and your typeface. It runs in an isolated frame so styles never leak.

Out of the box and yours to control

It detects product images automatically. You can restrict it to specific pages, use your own button, or hand-pick which products get it.

Broad coverage, curated live

The model covers the majority of fashion catalogues. On a live storefront, the button appears only on garments that render well.

Fabric that behaves like fabric

Powered by Decart’s real-time try-on model, the result moves with the shopper instead of waiting in a render queue.

One line, every device

Mobile and desktop are both supported once it is live on your store. The browser extension is desktop-only because it is only for previewing.

Social share opportunity

Free distribution, built in.

Shoppers post their try-on videos to social media, and your storefront rides along.
Distribution you didn’t pay for, in front of people who weren’t shopping yet.
